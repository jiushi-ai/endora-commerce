Built for high-performance deployment in space-constrained environments, the 0.71L LIVA Z11 PLUS combines embedded Intel® Core™ Ultra Processors with AI acceleration, support for up to 96GB DDR5 memory, quad 4K display output, and rich I/O connectivity. It’s the ultimate compact solution for edge computing, smart retail, medical diagnostics, and modern business productivity.

AI-Accelerated by Intel® Core™ Ultra Processors

Powered by Intel® Core™ Ultra processor (Series 1 & 2), the LIVA Z11 PLUS delivers up to 96 overall TOPS of AI performance with a built-in Neural Processing Unit (NPU) and Intel® Iris® Xe graphics. This platform offers seamless multitasking and enhanced responsiveness for workloads such as video encoding, intelligent automation, office productivity, and edge device management.

Superior Performance and High-Capacity Storage

Designed for demanding workloads, the LIVA Z11 PLUS delivers exceptional memory and storage performance in a compact footprint. It supports up to 96GB of dual-channel DDR5 memory—running at 5600MHz with Intel® Core™ Ultra processor (Series 1), or at blazing-fast 6400MHz with Series 2. Paired with dual NVMe Gen4x4 SSDs (M.2 2280 + M.2 2242), this configuration ensures accelerated boot-up, ultra-responsive multitasking, and smooth performance across AI-enhanced applications and modern business environments.

Boost Productivity with Quad 4K Display Output

Designed for advanced multitasking, LIVA Z11 PLUS enables simultaneous output to four 4K displays through two HDMI™ ports and two USB4 40Gbps Type-C (DisplayPort Alt Mode) connectors. This expanded visual workspace enhances operational efficiency and is ideal for digital signage, surveillance monitoring, financial dashboards, and content creation workflows.

Comprehensive I/O for Maximum Integration

Despite its compact size, LIVA Z11 PLUS offers extensive connectivity to support a wide range of devices. The front panel provides one USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 Type-C, three USB 3.2 Gen 2x1, and one USB 2.0 port for convenient high-speed access. On the rear, it includes two USB4 40Gbps Type-C and two USB 2.0 ports. Combined with HDMI™, and dual LAN, this rich I/O selection ensures smooth integration into diverse business, education, and commercial setups.

Versatile Applications Across AI and Commercial Fields

LIVA Z11 PLUS is purpose-built for a wide range of applications that demand compact size and high-performance computing. It supports AI-powered edge processing, real-time object detection, and medical imaging-assisted diagnostics. The system also excels in everyday business operations, retail POS and kiosk deployments, and digital classrooms, making it an ideal solution for commercial, healthcare, and educational environments.
